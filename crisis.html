Crisis & Care Disclaimer

Effective date: July 31, 2026

Anchored is a devotional — not counseling and not a crisis service

Anchored is a spiritual-growth and devotional tool. It offers Scripture, prayer prompts, reflection, and encouragement to help you build a daily discipline.

Anchored is NOT, and does not provide:

  • Medical, psychological, or psychiatric care or diagnosis
  • Professional counseling or therapy
  • Crisis intervention or emergency services
  • Pastoral counseling from a licensed or ordained minister
  • Any substitute for a doctor, licensed therapist, counselor, pastor, or emergency responder

Nothing in Anchored — including anything the automated Companion says — is professional advice. The Companion is an automated tool drawing on a fixed set of responses; it does not know you, cannot assess your safety, and is not a person. Do not rely on Anchored for any decision affecting your health or safety.

If you are in crisis, reach a real person now

If you are thinking about harming yourself or someone else, or you are in emotional or physical danger, please reach a real person right now — you do not have to carry it alone:

  • Call or text 988 — the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline (United States), available 24/7, free and confidential.
  • Text HOME to 741741 — the Crisis Text Line, 24/7.
  • Call 911 or go to your nearest emergency room if you are in immediate danger.
  • Outside the US: contact your local emergency number or a local crisis line.

If this is an emergency, contact emergency services first — before opening the app.

How Anchored handles hard moments

Anchored may notice patterns in your check-ins (for example, a stretch of low "how is your heart" ratings) and gently reach out. These messages are automated encouragement — not a clinical assessment, and not a judgment about your spiritual state. They will always point you to real help (988 / 741741 / 911) when the moment calls for it.

A message you send us is read by a person, not answered by a bot. If you reply to an Anchored email or write to support about something heavy — grief, doubt, despair, thoughts of self-harm — a human reviews it. We do not use an auto-responder to reply to a crisis message. Because we are a small team, we cannot guarantee an immediate response and we are not a monitored crisis line — if you need help now, use 988 / 741741 / 911.

No promise of outcomes

Faith and healing are personal. Anchored does not promise that using it will change your circumstances, your mental health, your relationships, or your spiritual life in any particular way. What we offer is a daily practice and encouragement — the rest is between you, your effort, and your faith.

Your acknowledgment

By using Anchored you acknowledge that you understand it is a devotional tool, not professional care or a crisis service, and that in an emergency you will contact 988, 741741, 911, or local emergency services rather than rely on the app.
