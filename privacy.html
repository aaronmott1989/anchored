Information we collect

  • Information you give us: your name and email address when you create an account or opt in; and — if you buy a plan or product — the purchase details our checkout provider collects to complete your order.
  • Faith and reflection data you enter in the app (only if you create an account): your questionnaire answers, goals, the things you tell us you're "carrying," your daily check-ins and mood/"how is your heart" ratings, and your private journal entries. We treat this as sensitive personal information.
  • Automatically collected information: IP address, browser and device type, pages viewed, referring source, and similar usage data, collected through cookies and analytics/advertising pixels (including the Meta pixel).

How your app data is stored today. During this early stage, the app runs on your device: your account, questionnaire, check-ins, mood ratings, and private journal are stored locally in your browser/device, not on a secure Anchored server, and the on-device "login" does not verify your password on a server. That means anyone with access to your device could reach this data, and it does not sync across devices. Please do not enter anything you need kept confidential until we launch secure, server-side accounts. We tell you this plainly inside the app, too.

How we use your information

  • To deliver the free resource you requested and to run your account and daily plan.
  • To send the email reminders and encouragement you opted in to, each with an easy way to stop.
  • To measure and improve our website, app, and advertising.
  • To comply with the law and protect against fraud or abuse.

We do not use your faith, journal, prayer, or mood information to target advertising, and we do not sell it.

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us operate, and only as needed:
