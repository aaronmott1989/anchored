Questions

Is it really free? +
Yes. Five short emails, one each morning for five days. No card, no cost, nothing to buy. Unsubscribe any time with one tap.
What if I miss a morning? +
Nothing to make up. There's no reset to zero and no catching up — His mercies are new every morning. You just open the next one when you can.
Do I need to be far along in faith? +
No. Each morning is short and plain, with the passage and a prayer written out for you. You only need a couple of quiet minutes and a willingness to show up.
Which Bible translation do I need? +
None of your own required — the passage is printed right in each email (public-domain World English Bible). Read along in your own Bible too if you like.
